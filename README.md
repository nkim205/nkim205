## 🎓 About Me
I am a computer science student at the Georgia Institute of Technology with a focus in information, networks, modeling, & simulation. I have a passion for building full stack applications that help tackle every day problems. I am looking to apply my knowledge in data focused, full stack development to make a positive impact to the world around me. 

## 📝 Projects & Experiences
#### 🏫 CSync: College Application Tracking Platform
A full stack React/Node web application that helps student manage, organize, and visualize their college application process with <100ms API response times, 99.9% uptime, and secure data encryption.

#### 🚀 Guidance, Navigations, & Controls Vice-Lead, Propulsive Landers @ Georgia Tech
Led development for a path planning algorithm that leveraged the strengths of both Hermite and Bézier splines to reduce computations by 50%. Co-led development for a full state space model along with a Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) controller. 

#### ⚡ Grid Resilience, Outage, Weather, Emergency Response Lab @ Georgia Tech
Integrated US census population data with weather service models to visualize zip code level high-risk outage zones through GeoPandas and Matplotlib.
